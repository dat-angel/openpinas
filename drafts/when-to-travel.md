# When to travel

Part of the OpenPinas diaspora resources guide. See also: getting-around-manila.md, banking-and-apps.md, packing-list.md.

## Seasons

- **Dry season, December–May.** PAGASA splits it into a *cool dry* season (December–February) and a *hot dry* season (March–May). The coolest month is usually January and the warmest is May. ([PAGASA: Climate of the Philippines](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/information/climate-philippines))
- **Rainy season, June–November.** ([PAGASA](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/information/climate-philippines))
- **Typhoons.** About 20 tropical cyclones enter the Philippine Area of Responsibility each year, and 8 or 9 of them cross the country. The peak runs July–October, when nearly 70% of typhoons develop. ([PAGASA: Tropical Cyclone Information](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/climate/tropical-cyclone-information)) The US State Dept says most typhoons hit between July and November but they can happen at any time. ([US State Dept: Philippines travel advisory and country information](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/philippines-travel-advisory.html))
- **Tip:** Rainfall varies a lot by region. Eastern coasts can get rain all year. ([PAGASA rainfall climatology, Villafuerte 2023](https://climgridph.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/climapv2/files/Villafuerte_2023.pdf)) Check PAGASA forecasts for your actual destination.

## Holidays and peak periods (2026)

All dates below come from **Proclamation No. 1006 (s. 2025)**. ([LawPhil text](https://lawphil.net/executive/proc/proc2025/proc_1006_2025.html); [PNA summary](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1258046))

| Period | 2026 dates | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Holy Week | Maundy Thursday Apr 2, Good Friday Apr 3 (regular holidays); Black Saturday Apr 4 (special non-working) | AirAsia moved its NAIA terminal "in time for the Holy Week travel peak" ([AirAsia Newsroom, Mar 28, 2026](https://newsroom.airasia.com/stories/2026/3/28/airasia-philippines-is-all-set-for-its-international-operations-transfer-to-naia-t1-in-time-for-the-holy-week-travel-peak)) |
| Undas (All Saints/All Souls) | Nov 1 (Sun) and Nov 2 (Mon), both special non-working | For Undas 2025, PITX bus terminal logged 194,000 passengers in one day, the season's peak ([GMA News, Oct 31, 2025](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/964395/influx-of-passengers-at-terminals-as-undas-2025-long-weekend-starts/story/)) |
| Christmas season | Dec 8 (special), Dec 24 (special), Dec 25 (regular), Dec 30 Rizal Day (regular), Dec 31 (special) | Expect crowded airports and roads in late December |
| Simbang Gabi | Dawn Masses Dec 16–24 | Nine consecutive dawn Masses, also called Misa de Gallo or Aguinaldo Masses ([CBCP News](https://cbcpnews.net/cbcpnews/instructions-on-the-celebration-of-aguinaldo-masses-misa-de-gallo-or-simbang-gabi/)) |
| Other 2026 holidays | Jan 1, Feb 17 (Chinese New Year, special), Apr 9, May 1, Jun 12, Aug 21 (special), Aug 31, Nov 30 | Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha dates are proclaimed separately ([Proc. 1006](https://lawphil.net/executive/proc/proc2025/proc_1006_2025.html)) |

- **UNVERIFIED for 2027:** At the time of research, no 2027 holiday proclamation had been checked. Update this table once Malacañang releases it.
