# Arrival and getting around Metro Manila

## Before you land: eTravel

- **Who must register:** Arriving Filipino and foreign passengers and crew, and departing Filipino passengers. ([eTravel FAQ](https://etravel.gov.ph/en/frequently-asked-questions))
- **When:** Register within 72 hours before arrival or departure, at [etravel.gov.ph](https://etravel.gov.ph/) or in the eGovPH app. ([eTravel FAQ](https://etravel.gov.ph/en/frequently-asked-questions); [Bureau of Immigration advisory](https://immigration.gov.ph/advisory-etravel-registration/))
- **Cost: free.** Scam sites have charged travelers. BI reported fees of P3,000–P5,000 collected by fake sites. Use only the official site. ([Bureau of Immigration](https://immigration.gov.ph/etravel-registration-free-of-charge/))
- **Save the QR code** (screenshot or download it) and show it to the airline before boarding. A green QR means you go straight to immigration; a red QR means extra Quarantine checks first. ([eTravel FAQ](https://etravel.gov.ph/en/frequently-asked-questions))

## NAIA terminals

- **Terminal 1:** Mostly international carriers, including Philippine Airlines international, Korean Air, EVA Air, and Saudia. Since Mar 29, 2026 it also handles AirAsia Philippines international flights. ([NNIC airline directory](https://www.newnaia.com.ph/flights/airline-information); [AirAsia Newsroom](https://newsroom.airasia.com/stories/2026/2/25/airasia-philippines-to-operate-all-international-flights-at-naia-terminal-1-starting-march-29))
- **Terminal 2:** Domestic only, for PAL, PAL Express, and AirAsia domestic. ([NNIC airline directory](https://www.newnaia.com.ph/flights/airline-information); [AirAsia Newsroom](https://newsroom.airasia.com/stories/2026/2/25/airasia-philippines-to-operate-all-international-flights-at-naia-terminal-1-starting-march-29))
- **Terminal 3:** Cebu Pacific plus many foreign airlines, including United, Emirates, Qatar, Singapore, Cathay, ANA, and Air Canada. Air China, China Eastern, Vietnam Airlines, Royal Brunei, and Shenzhen Airlines moved here on Mar 29, 2026, and JAL moved on Apr 1, 2026. ([GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/money/companies/982065/list-naia-terminal-reassignments-for-foreign-airlines/story/); [NNIC airline directory](https://www.newnaia.com.ph/flights/airline-information))
- **Assignments change.** Always confirm the terminal on your booking. ([GMA News, quoting NNIC](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/money/companies/982065/list-naia-terminal-reassignments-for-foreign-airlines/story/))
- **Terminals are not connected.** The free **NAIA Inter-Terminal Shuttle** runs 24/7 between T1, T2, and T3. It leaves about every 15 minutes at peak and every 30 minutes from 12–2am. Priority goes to passengers with connecting flights or who were dropped at the wrong terminal, so show your boarding pass or booking. ([Top Gear PH, Aug 13, 2026](https://www.topgear.com.ph/features/feature-articles/naia-inter-terminal-shuttle-route-schedules-more-a4682-20260813))
- **International terminal fee:** Reported as P950 since Sep 14, 2025, with OFWs exempt. **UNVERIFIED against an official page:** the NNIC news page would not render for verification. Secondary sources: [AirportsPH](https://airportsph.com/naia-terminal-finder/), [NNIC news item](https://newnaia.com.ph/about-us/news/naia-operator-says-fee-hikes-mandated-by-govt-ofws-exempt).

## Airport transfers

- **Ride-hailing apps** are safe and widely used in Metro Manila, per the US State Dept. Availability drops at rush hour, on holidays, late at night, and in heavy rain. ([US State Dept](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/philippines-travel-advisory.html))
- **Taxi scams:** Airport taxi drivers sometimes overcharge or refuse to use the meter. Insist on the meter and take a photo of the plate. ([US State Dept](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/philippines-travel-advisory.html)) In 2026, LTO permanently revoked the license of a driver who charged a foreigner P2,000 for a five-minute ride. ([Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/features/feature-articles/naia-inter-terminal-shuttle-route-schedules-more-a4682-20260813))
- **Yellow airport taxi flag-down:** P115, up from P75, effective Mar 19, 2026. Regular-taxi and motorcycle-taxi fare petitions were still pending at that time. ([GMA News, Mar 17, 2026](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/980266/ltfrb-approves-fare-hike-in-almost-all-public-utility-vehicles/story/))
- **TNVS (Grab-type) base fares from Mar 19, 2026:** Sedan P65, premium P165. ([GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/980266/ltfrb-approves-fare-hike-in-almost-all-public-utility-vehicles/story/))
- **UBE Express P2P bus (as of Aug 8, 2026):**
  - NAIA–PITX: P150
  - NAIA–Robinsons Santa Rosa: P300
  - NAIA terminal loop: P50, 4am–8pm
  - Boarding at T3 Arrivals, Bay 12. Pay with cash or Beep. Several other routes (Cubao, Robinsons Manila, Alabang, Imus, Victory Liner Pasay) were **temporarily suspended**.
  - ([The Poor Traveler, updated Aug 8, 2026](https://www.thepoortraveler.net/ube-express-bus-naia/))
  - Check UBE's official channels before you go: [ubeexpress.com](https://www.ubeexpress.com/).

## Trains: LRT-1, LRT-2, MRT-3 and Beep

- **Beep card:** Works on all three lines, many P2P buses, and some jeepneys. Buy at any LRT/MRT station (teller or ticket machine), and load at stations, in the beep™ app, or through GCash, Maya, or BPI. Maximum load is P10,000. The card is valid 4 years and can be extended 1 year. Load on a lost card can't be recovered. ([beep™ FAQ](https://beep.com.ph/faqs/))
- **Tap-to-pay with a bank card:**
  - **MRT-3:** Since Jul 25, 2025, accepts Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards, GCash QR, and NFC Android phones. The fare is the same as a single-journey ticket. ([GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/serbisyopubliko/transportation/953734/mastercard-cardholders-can-now-tap-their-cards-to-pay-mrt-3-fares/story/))
  - **LRT-2:** Since Jul 13, 2026, accepts Visa/Mastercard debit, credit, or prepaid cards. ([GMA News, Jul 13, 2026](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/money/companies/994654/you-can-now-tap-or-scan-to-pay-for-lrt-2-trips/story/))
  - **LRT-1: UNVERIFIED.** Open-loop card payment was reported as "planned" (Manila Bulletin, Jul 13, 2026, page blocked to the fetcher). Carry a Beep card for LRT-1.
- **Foreign cards on the gates: UNVERIFIED.** The sources list Philippine-issued cards. Whether foreign-issued Visa/Mastercard cards work at the gates wasn't confirmed.

## Motorcycle taxis

- Angkas, JoyRide, and Move It are LTFRB-accredited. The Metro Manila rider cap is 45,000 (Angkas 23,164; JoyRide 15,000; Move It 6,836). ([BusinessWorld, Jul 30, 2026](https://bworldonline.com/the-nation/2026/07/30/767124/ltfrb-opens-motorcycle-taxi-bids/))
- **UNVERIFIED:** Whether a permanent motorcycle-taxi law has passed. Reports say it is still under an interim framework.

## Driving: number coding (UVVRP)

- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 7–10am and 5–8pm, except holidays. ([DZRH, quoting MMDA, Jan 5, 2026](https://www.dzrh.com.ph/post/mmda-reminds-motorists-of-expanded-number-coding-hours-in-metro-manila))
- **Banned plate endings:** Mon 1–2, Tue 3–4, Wed 5–6, Thu 7–8, Fri 9–0. TNVS, PUVs, and motorcycles are exempt. ([DZRH/MMDA](https://www.dzrh.com.ph/post/mmda-reminds-motorists-of-expanded-number-coding-hours-in-metro-manila))
- **Foreign licenses:** A US license is recognized, with restrictions. ([US State Dept](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/philippines-travel-advisory.html))
- **UNVERIFIED:** The rule that a foreign license is valid for 90 days after arrival. It's commonly cited, but the [DFA Berne FAQ](https://bernepe.dfa.gov.ph/other-information/frequently-asked-questions/driving-in-the-philippines) was blocked during research. Confirm with LTO.
