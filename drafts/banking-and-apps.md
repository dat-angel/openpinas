# Banking, money and apps

## Cash, cards and ATMs

- **Bringing cash in or out:**
  - More than **PHP 50,000** in pesos needs prior BSP authorization.
  - More than **US$10,000** (or equivalent) in foreign currency must be declared to Customs.
  - ([US State Dept, Philippines country information](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/philippines-travel-advisory.html); BSP FAQ PDF was blocked to the fetcher: [BSP cross-border FAQ](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Lists/Download%20Section/Attachments/149/faqcrossbordertransfer.pdf))
- **Cards:** Larger restaurants and hotels often take credit cards. Small shops and taxis are usually cash-only. Most ATMs accept US debit cards, but withdrawal limits can be low. ([US State Dept](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/philippines-travel-advisory.html))
- **ATM fees, example:** BDO charges **P250 per withdrawal** on cards issued overseas, plus whatever your home bank charges (as published Oct 2026). The maximum is P25,000 per transaction. ([BDO ATMs](https://www.bdo.com.ph/personal/digital/atm)) The US State Dept notes total ATM fees can exceed US$5 per transaction. ([US State Dept](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/philippines-travel-advisory.html))
- **Fraud:** Card skimming at ATMs is common. Use ATMs inside banks or malls. ([US State Dept](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/philippines-travel-advisory.html))
- **UNVERIFIED:** Fees at other banks (BPI, Metrobank, and others) for foreign cards weren't confirmed from official pages.

## E-wallets: GCash and Maya

- **GCash Overseas** lets you use GCash with a foreign mobile number in supported countries, including the US, Canada, UK, Australia, Japan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and many others.
  - Features include sending to PH GCash users, InstaPay transfers to PH banks, paying PH bills, buying PH load, and cashing in via PayPal, Payoneer, or remittance partners.
  - ([GCash Help: Overseas countries and services](https://help.gcash.com/hc/en-us/articles/28753833785241-GCash-Overseas-countries-and-services))
  - **UNVERIFIED:** Eligibility details (for example, Filipino citizen, 18+, PH government ID). The [GCash Overseas sign-up article](https://help.gcash.com/hc/en-us/articles/28752622792089-How-to-create-a-GCash-Overseas-account) was bot-blocked during research.
- **Maya** advertises remittance delivery into Maya wallets and cash-out at Maya Center locations. ([Maya: International Remittances](https://www.maya.ph/ofw))
  - **UNVERIFIED:** Who can open Maya Savings from abroad (for example, whether US persons are excluded). Check the [Maya Bank support article](https://support.mayabank.ph/s/article/Who-can-open-a-Maya-Savings-account), which is JS-rendered and couldn't be read.
- **On trains:** GCash QR works at MRT-3 and LRT-2 gates ([GCash QR on MRT-3, GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/serbisyopubliko/transportation/953734/mastercard-cardholders-can-now-tap-their-cards-to-pay-mrt-3-fares/story/); [LRT-2, GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/money/companies/994654/you-can-now-tap-or-scan-to-pay-for-lrt-2-trips/story/)). GCash, Maya, and BPI can also top up Beep cards. ([beep™ FAQ](https://beep.com.ph/faqs/))

## Sending money home (remittances)

- **Options:**
  - Cash into a family member's GCash via international remittance partners or PayPal ([GCash Help](https://help.gcash.com/hc/en-us/articles/28753833785241-GCash-Overseas-countries-and-services))
  - Send to a Maya wallet ([Maya](https://www.maya.ph/ofw))
  - Bank-to-bank transfer through your bank's remittance service

## SIM cards and eSIMs

- **The law:** The SIM Registration Act (RA 11934) requires every SIM, **including eSIMs**, to be registered before it is activated. Registration is free. ([RA 11934, LawPhil](https://www.lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11934_2022.html))
- **Tourists (9(a) visa)** register with their passport, proof of a Philippine address, and a return or onward ticket. These SIMs are **valid for only 30 days** and then deactivate automatically. ([RA 11934, Sec. 5(e)](https://www.lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11934_2022.html))
  - **Editorial note:** Balikbayans entering visa-free on a foreign passport have a different status. Which documents a telco accepts from them is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Get an eSIM before you fly:** The US State Dept recommends a travel eSIM bought before departure, because SIM registration can slow down buying a SIM at the airport. ([US State Dept](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/philippines-travel-advisory.html))
- **Globe Prepaid Traveler eSIM:** **P1,750** for 80GB of data plus unlimited local calls and texts, valid 30 days (as listed Oct 2026).
  - Buy it in the GlobeOne app. You must **register after landing**, because the OTP only arrives on Globe's PH network.
  - Your phone must be carrier-unlocked. No refunds.
  - ([Globe Help: Prepaid Traveler eSIM](https://www.globe.com.ph/help/international/prepaid-traveler-esim))
- **Smart:** See [Smart's guide for tourists](https://blog.smart.com.ph/are-tourists-foreign-nationals-required-to-register-their-sim/). **UNVERIFIED:** Smart eSIM pricing.
